02 / PEOPLE, JUDGEMENT & PROFESSIONAL SUPPORT

My experience.
Three settings.

The work has changed. The underlying responsibility has not: understanding what someone needs and responding carefully.

01 / 2021–2022

Kumon

Supporting students with different learning needs taught me to listen before deciding what help would work. I tracked progress, updated my supervisor and spoke with parents about areas requiring support. I also became more willing to adjust my own study methods during the HSC rather than judge myself by immediate results.

LISTENING · ADAPTABILITY · CLEAR EXPLANATIONS
02 / 2023–2025

Guzman y Gomez

Busy shifts taught me to organise competing orders, respond to customer concerns and communicate when priorities suddenly changed. I stopped treating asking for help as a failure of independence. I learned that reliable service often depends on timely updates and knowing when to involve others.

SERVICE · PRIORITISATION · COLLABORATION
03 / 2026–PRESENT

Legal Assistant

In personal injury practice, I handle client enquiries, files, diary commitments and time-sensitive correspondence. I have become more careful about keeping records accurate and asking questions early, because another person may be relying on the information I provide. Working and studying full-time has also made me more deliberate about planning ahead.

DISCRETION · RECORDS · ACCOUNTABILITY
THE SKILLS WERE TESTED AGAIN

From helping one person to supporting a whole team.

MQBS3010 gave me a way to put those habits to work in a more complex setting. When our Hillview proposal brought together seven disciplines and different schedules, listening, clear updates and accurate information stopped being individual strengths and became necessary for other people to progress.

I drew on my legal administration experience when organising the HR contribution and on the adaptability I developed at Kumon and GYG when sharing feedback across sections. I also recognised a limitation: I could respond to a problem, but I did not always anticipate it early enough. That is why my later reflection focuses on clearer ownership, earlier check-ins and measurable handovers.

HOW I WOULD APPLY THIS AT MORAY & AGNEW

Support that is accurate, timely and considerate.

The People & Development Assistant role involves employee enquiries, onboarding administration and keeping important information organised. Those responsibilities call for the same care I've practised with parents, customers and legal clients, refined through coordinating our Capstone work. I would focus on understanding the enquiry, recording it correctly, recognising when guidance is needed and following through so the next person can act with confidence.
