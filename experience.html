02 / EMPLOYEE ENQUIRIES & PROFESSIONAL COMMUNICATION

People need more
than an answer.

Whether speaking with a student, a customer or a legal client, I have learned that how you communicate matters as much as what you communicate.

THE ROLE REQUIRES

Clear, thoughtful responses to people.

At Moray & Agnew, employee queries need an accurate answer, but also someone who listens, recognises when a matter needs clarification and follows through. This is a responsibility I've practised in different environments.

JOB LISTING / EMPLOYEE QUERIES · SERVICE · ADMINISTRATION
01
LISTENING AND ADAPTING / KUMON

Understanding what someone needs before deciding how to help.

I taught children, recorded their progress, maintained learning records and updated my supervisor about when students needed extra support or were ready to move levels. Speaking with parents taught me to explain difficult topics with care.

Supporting students with different learning needs changed how I viewed my own academic progress. I began identifying weaknesses in my HSC studies and adapting my approach rather than treating difficulty as a lack of ability.

02
PRIORITISING AND COMMUNICATING / GUZMAN Y GOMEZ

Staying useful when priorities change.

During busy shifts, I managed orders, payments, phone enquiries and customer concerns. I learned to prioritise under pressure and recognise when seeking help was more responsible than trying to handle everything alone.

RELEVANT TO / RESPONSIVE SERVICE · COLLABORATION · COMPETING TASKS

03
PRECISION AND DISCRETION / LEGAL ASSISTANT

Information people can rely on.

In personal injury practice, I draft letters of advice, respond to client enquiries, maintain files and diaries and manage time-sensitive administration. I have become deliberate about accurate records, seeking clarification early and keeping others informed.

Legal deadlines carry a different weight when a delay can affect someone's claim. My understanding of responsibility now includes anticipating how my work affects the next person relying on it.

WHAT THIS MEANS FOR PEOPLE & DEVELOPMENT

Listen carefully. Record accurately. Ask early.

These are the habits I would bring to employee enquiries and administrative support at Moray & Agnew. They have also shaped how I approach teamwork and people development in my Capstone work.
