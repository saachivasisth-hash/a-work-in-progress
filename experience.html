01 / Experience

What work has
taught me.

Each role involved a new environment, new expectations and a new way to understand the people around me.

2021—2022 / Education

Kumon · Teaching and learning support

I taught children, monitored their progress, kept records and spoke with parents. When students fell behind, I needed to identify the issue, explain it sensitively and help adjust their learning level.

What challenged me

Entering the workforce while still at school and learning to communicate about a student's difficulties—not just their achievements.

What stays with me

Support works best when we notice differences in people's needs instead of expecting everyone to progress in the same way.

2023—2025 / Hospitality

Guzman y Gomez · Crew Member

I handled orders, payments, discounts, telephone enquiries, drive-through service and customer concerns. During busy shifts I collaborated with colleagues and helped delegate tasks.

What challenged me

Finding a job after high school, adjusting to my first customer-facing role and working in a fast-moving environment where priorities changed quickly.

What stays with me

Communication is operational: when one person's update is missing, the whole team's work becomes harder.

2026—Present / Legal practice

Paralegal · Legal administration and client service

My responsibilities include drafting letters of advice, speaking with clients, answering concerns, diarising, coordinating bookings, preparing file notes and supporting administrative workflows.

What challenged me

Moving into a professional legal environment while studying full-time and working full-time. The stakes of accuracy, professionalism and deadlines feel different from my previous roles.

What stays with me

Being responsive matters, but so do sound judgement, reliable records and knowing when something needs to be clarified.

The thread connecting these experiences

I used to think being capable meant getting my own tasks done. Increasingly, I understand it as helping other people move forward too.

These roles have given me experience across different workplace cultures, although I am still building formal HR expertise. I'm particularly interested in how People & Development teams create clarity, support learning and help employees navigate day-to-day work.
