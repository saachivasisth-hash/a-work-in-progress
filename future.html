Development plan
0–6

Months · Get the foundations right

Learn onboarding, offboarding, HR record-keeping and confidentiality expectations under supervision. Track accuracy, timeliness and supervisor feedback on assigned processes.

6–12

Months · Become more confident with systems

Build practical skills using HR information systems and workforce spreadsheets. Request feedback on process coordination and take part in relevant internal training.

12–24

Months · Contribute to better employee experiences

Support a learning, onboarding or employee experience improvement project and assess its value through feedback and clear operational measures.

Why this role makes sense for me

I understand the realities of working in a legal environment, but I'm curious about supporting the people who make that work possible.

My experience has not followed a single straight path. Each role challenged me to adapt and each has made me more interested in how workplaces support learning, coordination and growth. I see a People & Development Assistant role as an opportunity to turn those transferable skills into purposeful HR practice.
