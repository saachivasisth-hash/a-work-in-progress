MY PORTFOLIO RESPONSE TO

MORAY & AGNEW

People & Development Assistant / Sydney / Hybrid. A role that brings together what I study in HR with what I've learned working in legal practice.

WHY THIS ROLE?

It sits at the intersection of my degree and the work I'm already doing.

The job listing emphasises responding to employee queries, accurate administration, onboarding and learning support. What interests me most is the opportunity to work behind the scenes to make employees' experiences at work easier and more organised. Working as a Legal Assistant has shown me how much people rely on clear communication, accurate information and timely support, particularly in a busy legal environment. Combined with my HR studies, this has made me interested in how employees are supported from the moment they join an organisation and throughout their development.

WHAT MORAY & AGNEW NEEDSHOW I'VE DEMONSTRATED ITHOW I'D APPLY IT

Employee enquiries and relationships

Communicating with parents, customers and legal clients; listening and clarifying issues in Team 4K.

Respond clearly, seek clarification when needed and follow through so employees receive reliable support.

Accurate people administration

Maintaining legal records, diary deadlines and a documented HR staffing comparison.

Handle employee information carefully and use checked processes for onboarding and lifecycle administration.

Learning and development support

Designing mentoring into a role description and evaluating the value of training through Harvest Pathways and Green Skills Academy.

Help coordinate learning activities and consider whether employees gain usable skills from them.

Teamwork and competing deadlines

Teams coordination, role allocation and critical evaluation of our Team 4K agility debrief.

Communicate task ownership early, track milestones and seek feedback on handovers.

MY FIRST 90 DAYS / IF APPOINTED

Learn the process. Improve the experience.

01 / LISTEN

Observe employee enquiry and onboarding workflows and ask colleagues where delays or errors most often arise.

02 / DOCUMENT

Develop a simple checklist for recurring tasks, with a supervisor checking its accuracy.

03 / TEST

Seek feedback on accuracy, timeliness and clarity, then adjust how I manage the next set of handovers.

I would bring an understanding of legal workplaces, experience supporting different people and the habit of taking feedback seriously. I want to apply those skills to help employees feel informed and supported throughout their time at the firm.
