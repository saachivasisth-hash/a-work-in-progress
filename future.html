MY FIRST 90 DAYS / IF APPOINTED

A practical learning plan.

01 / LISTEN

Observe onboarding and employee-query workflows; ask colleagues how they prefer updates and what errors recur.

02 / DOCUMENT

Build a personal process checklist for recurring HR tasks, checking its accuracy with a supervisor.

03 / TEST

Seek feedback on accuracy, timeliness and clarity. Revise the checklist and compare the next set of handovers.
