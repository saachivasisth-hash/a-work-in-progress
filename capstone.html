Strategic workforce planning for Hillview Estate taught me that an inclusive idea is only useful if an organisation can put it into practice.

The King's Foundation AustraliaInterdisciplinary teamworkSDG 8
My contribution

Developing people, sustainably.

Our team worked on a proposed sustainable vineyard at Hillview Estate. My contribution focused on workforce planning, inclusive recruitment and employee development. I explored a proposed Harvest Pathways Program and Green Skills Academy.

The tension

Ambition versus feasibility.

Training pathways and inclusive hiring matter. But seasonal labour needs, staffing costs and the organisation's stage of development shape what is possible.

What changed in my thinking
01

Start with the person.

My HR perspective naturally focused on access, capability-building and decent employment.

02

Listen to other disciplines.

Finance projections, economic constraints, marketing demand and operational requirements challenged me to look beyond whether an initiative sounded beneficial.

03

Make the proposal workable.

I began seeing workforce development as a phased decision, where staffing and learning initiatives must align with resources and demand.
